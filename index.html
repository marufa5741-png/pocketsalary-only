Why this number is correct

PF = 12% of Basic • Gratuity = 4.81% of Basic • Standard Deduction = Rs 75,000 • PF ceiling Rs 25,000 from 17 Sep 2026. Every line above shows its own arithmetic — tap to verify.

What changed in 2026

PF ceiling: Rs 25,000

Capped PF is now Rs 3,000/month (was Rs 1,800). Source: PIB, 16 Sep 2026 — S.O. 5109(E).

12.75 Lakh = Zero Tax

12.75L − 75k = 12L taxable. Rebate under Sec 156 = Rs 60,000. Net tax Rs 0.

50% wage rule

Basic must be at least 50% of pay from 21 Nov 2025. Excess allowances added back to wages.
