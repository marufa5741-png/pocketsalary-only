Important Rule: 12 Lakh Zero Tax

If your taxable income after ₹75,000 standard deduction is up to ₹12,00,000, you get full rebate u/s 87A = ₹60,000, so Net Tax = ₹0.

Example: Gross 12.75L - 75k Std Ded = 12L Taxable → Tax before rebate = ₹60,000 → Rebate = ₹60,000 → Net Tax = 0

If taxable income exceeds ₹12,00,000 by even ₹1, you lose full rebate and pay tax from ₹4L onwards (marginal relief available).
